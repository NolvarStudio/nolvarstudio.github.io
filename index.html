Privacy notice

Who we are. Nolvar Studio, run by Mario Gjurra. Contact: mario at nolvarstudio dot com.

What we collect. Business contact details of people at companies we may work with: name, job title, company, business email address and the company's public ads. We get them from public sources such as company websites, LinkedIn and Meta's Ad Library, and from business contact databases.

Why. To contact businesses about our ad creative services. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in business-to-business marketing (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We send a few relevant messages and stop when asked.

How long. Up to 12 months after our last contact, unless you become a client.

Who else sees it. Our email and outreach providers process it on our behalf. We don't sell data.

Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to being contacted, by replying to any of our emails or writing to mario at nolvarstudio dot com. Opt-outs are handled the same day. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
